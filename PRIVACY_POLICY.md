# Privacy Policy

**App:** StudyFlow
**Package:** com.oldalexhub.studyflow
**Last updated:** May 13, 2026

---

## Overview

StudyFlow is a simple study timer app. It runs entirely on your device. It does not connect to the internet, does not have a backend server, and does not collect, store, or share any information about you or your usage.

---

## What data we collect

**None.**

StudyFlow does not collect any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, analytics, crash reports, or any other information of any kind.

---

## Internet access

StudyFlow does not make any network requests. The app works fully offline and does not communicate with any external server, API, or third-party service.

---

## Third-party services

StudyFlow does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting services.

---

## Data storage

StudyFlow stores nothing. There is no account system, no login, no local database, and no persistent storage of any kind. When you close the app, no record of your session is kept anywhere.

---

## Children's privacy

Because StudyFlow collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data — this document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
